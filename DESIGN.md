## Brand & Style

The design system embodies a "Maximalist Minimalist" philosophy, specifically engineered for a Gen Z audience. It balances the sophisticated, editorial honesty of a premium pantry brand with the high-octane energy of modern street food culture. The visual language is simple yet loud, science-backed yet vibrant.

The chosen style is **High-Contrast / Bold**. It utilizes massive, chunky typography and a "visual punch" approach to information architecture. Whitespace is used not just for breathing room, but as a high-contrast stage for saturated brand colors and high-fidelity product photography. The emotional response is one of immediate craving, unwavering trust in ingredient quality, and a sense of "premium accessible" luxury.

## Colors

The palette is a clash between "Premium Earthy" and "High-Energy Bold." 

- **Primary (Jing Red):** A deep, saturated red used for high-impact calls to action and hero backgrounds.
- **Secondary (Electric Yellow):** A vibrant yellow for highlights, badges, and secondary buttons to drive energy.
- **Neutral (Brightland Cream):** The "off-white" foundation that elevates the brand, preventing it from looking like a budget snack.
- **Deep Ink:** Used for all typography and structural borders to maintain readability and a grounded, scientific feel.

The color mode is strictly **light**, utilizing the cream base to ensure a warm, inviting, and organic feel that pure white cannot achieve.

## Typography

Typography is the primary driver of the brand's "vibrant science" personality.

- **Headlines:** Use a bold, condensed, and impactful sans-serif (Anton) to deliver the "visual punch." Headlines should be oversized and tightly tracked.
- **Body:** Use a clean, wide, and neutral geometric sans-serif (DM Sans) for maximum legibility. This balances the aggression of the headlines with professional clarity.
- **Labels/Technical Data:** Use a monospaced font (Space Mono) for ingredients, nutritional callouts, and "science-backed" data points to evoke a sense of precision and transparency.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses a **Fixed Grid** on desktop (12 columns) and a **Fluid Grid** on mobile (4 columns).

- **Grid Strategy:** Large elements should span 6 or 12 columns. Product cards are organized in a 3 or 4-column rhythm.
- **Spacing Rhythm:** Use large vertical gaps (120px+) between sections to allow the high-energy visuals to breathe. 
- **Mobile Reflow:** Content should stack vertically with generous 20px margins. Hero images should transition from landscape/background to portrait/full-bleed to maintain visual impact on small screens.
- **Visual Structure:** Follow the wireframe guide of using high-impact hero sections followed immediately by icon-driven benefit grids to quickly communicate value propositions.

## Elevation & Depth

To maintain the "Modern Minimalist" aesthetic, depth is achieved through **Bold Borders** and **Tonal Layers** rather than soft shadows.

- **Surfaces:** Use the secondary yellow and primary red as flat, layered surfaces to create hierarchy.
- **Outlines:** Use 2px "Deep Ink" borders for cards and containers to create a structured, "unrefined" look that feels intentional and premium.
- **Flat Depth:** Use hard, 100% opacity "drop shadows" (offsets of 4px or 8px in Deep Ink) behind buttons or featured cards to create a tactile, Gen Z-friendly pop-art feel.

## Shapes

The shape language is primarily **Soft** with 4px corners for a disciplined, structured look. 

- **Primary Buttons:** Should use a "Pill-shape" (3) to contrast against the rigid grid and provide a friendly, clickable target.
- **Product Cards/Images:** Use a subtle "Soft" (1) radius. This prevents the design from feeling too sharp/aggressive while maintaining the "clean product grid" aesthetic.
- **Special Callouts:** Starbursts or jagged "sticker" shapes (inspired by Fly By Jing) can be used for "New" or "Best Seller" badges to inject energy.

## Components

- **Buttons:** Primary buttons are large, high-contrast (Yellow on Red or Red on Cream), pill-shaped, and use the Anton font for the label. They should feature a 2px black border.
- **Icon-Driven Benefits:** Icons should be thick-stroke, monochromatic (Deep Ink) illustrations. They are paired with Space Mono labels and Anton sub-headings.
- **Product Cards:** Clean cream backgrounds with 2px borders. Product photography should be "floating" or high-contrast studio shots. Hover states should trigger the hard offset shadow.
- **Input Fields:** Rectangular with "Soft" corners, 2px Deep Ink borders, and DM Sans for placeholder text.
- **Chips/Badges:** Small, high-energy tags using Space Mono for technical callouts (e.g., "15G PROTEIN") or Starburst shapes for marketing callouts.
- **Clean Grids:** Use a 1px or 2px divider line (Deep Ink) between list items or grid sections to maintain the "honest and transparent" scientific layout.
